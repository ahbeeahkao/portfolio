# Bridal website reference review

## Scope and method

Reviewed the live homepages of ten official bridal businesses—five in Malaysia and five in Singapore—on 28 October 2026. The review considered their visible home-page structure, image-led sections, navigation/content hierarchy, and how their actual service scope is explained. This is a design-pattern and category-scope review, **not** a screenshot-to-screenshot benchmark, a ranking, or a claim that the concepts represent these businesses. Some page fetches expose structured text and image references rather than a full browser rendering, so fine-grained visual details (exact type, colors, motion, and responsive states) were not verified from screenshots.

Discovery roundups are shortlist sources only, not definitive rankings: [Wedding Notebook — Malaysia/KL and Singapore guide](https://theweddingnotebook.com/inspire/fashion-and-beauty/best-bridal-boutique-in-kl-singapore), [Wedding Notebook — Singapore guide](https://theweddingnotebook.com/inspire/fashion-and-beauty/best-bridal-boutiques-in-singapore), [SingaporeBrides directory](https://singaporebrides.com/featured/wedding-dresses-singapore/), and [Bridely directory](https://www.bridely.sg/bridal-shops).

## How this informed the seven concepts

The concepts borrow only broad, non-exclusive information-architecture patterns: distinct cultural/event wardrobes, curated collections, practical appointment planning, service/package comparisons, maker/craft storytelling, and accessible fitting or enquiry steps. They do not reuse reference-site names, copy, logos, photography, pricing, policies, testimonials, or identity. The fictional brand names and all sample commercial details remain clearly disclosed as concept-only.

The seven nav treatments are intentionally different: dark ceremonial horizontal masthead (Chinese), centered link row (Malay), left rail (Indian), fixed bottom dock (general), right-side rail (Kuala Lumpur), native disclosure menu (Johor), and two-tier tab strip (Penang). The homepage cards embed each local concept homepage in a miniature browser frame so visitors can identify the work as a website preview; selecting a card opens the full concept site.

## Limitations

The named shortlist pages are discovery material, not a definitive top-ten ranking. Page fetches were used to inspect official homepage structure and content cues, but this repository does not contain reference-site screenshots or a pixel-level side-by-side visual audit. Reference assets were not copied into the concepts. The Wix Studio handoff remains a manual-recreation specification only; no Wix build or 1:1 fidelity comparison has been completed.
